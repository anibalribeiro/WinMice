WinMice

Windows-style mouse on Mac

A tiny native menu-bar utility for middle-click vector scrolling and configurable back/forward side buttons. About 600 KB. No Electron.

Download Homebrew

Apple Silicon · macOS 14+

What it does

Two mouse behaviors Mac never quite matched — until now.

Middle-click vector scroll

Click the middle button, move away from the anchor, and scroll by direction and distance — Hold to Scroll or Hold to Start.

Back and forward side buttons

Map buttons 4 and 5 (or your own) to system-wide swipe or ⌘[ / ⌘] navigation, on press or release.

Why it’s tiny

Native Swift and AppKit. One lightweight menu-bar process. No bundled runtime.

Get it

Pick a path — both are first-class.

Download latest release

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later) running macOS 14 or later. Open the DMG, drag WinMice.app to /Applications, then open it. Grant Accessibility when asked. Release builds are Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple.
